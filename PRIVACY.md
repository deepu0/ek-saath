# Privacy Policy - EkSaath

Effective: 2026-08-21

This extension does not collect, transmit, or sell any data.

- Access: Reads tab URLs only to extract hostname for grouping. No URLs stored externally.
- Storage: Uses chrome.storage.local for settings (groupByRoot, autoReorder, scopeAllWindows, dedupe on/off, whitelist), the last tab order for Undo, and the URLs of tabs it closed for up to 30 seconds so Undo can reopen them. chrome.storage.session briefly remembers the ids of newly opened tabs (cleared when the browser closes). Nothing leaves the device.
- No network requests, no analytics, no third-party scripts. Fonts are bundled with the extension (since 1.2.1), so even the popup loads nothing from the internet.
- No personal data processed.
